What makes it different

Gratitude that grows with you

Most people cycle through the same handful of things they're thankful for. FindThanks gently challenges that habit.

📓

Three notes, every day

A focused daily ritual. Three entries keep the practice sustainable without feeling like a chore.

🔍

Repetition detection

Notes are stored as semantic embeddings. Before you submit, Check surfaces similar things you've already been grateful for.

📅

Calendar history

Browse every past entry on a visual calendar. See which days you showed up and revisit what mattered then.

🔒

Private by default

Your notes are yours. Data is stored securely and used only to power the app's core features — nothing else.

The workflow

Simple by design

1

Write three gratitude notes

Each day, write three things you're thankful for. Keep them specific — the more concrete, the more meaningful.

2

Hit Check before submitting

The app compares your notes against your entire history using semantic similarity, not just keyword matching. It will flag notes that are conceptually similar to past ones.

3

Refine or submit

If a note looks familiar, you're free to dig deeper — or submit as-is. No pressure, just awareness.

4

Build a richer picture over time

Over days and weeks your gratitude map expands. Revisit past entries on the calendar and watch your perspective grow.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 5, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including when you create an account, use our services, or contact us for support. This includes your gratitude notes, which are stored as text and as vector embeddings to enable similarity detection.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Detect semantic similarity between your current and past gratitude notes
  • Sync your notes across devices linked to your account
  • Send technical notices and support messages
  • Respond to your comments and questions

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from loss, theft, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. Your notes are stored securely and are never shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or permanently delete your personal information at any time. Please contact us if you wish to exercise these rights and we will respond promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a revised date.
